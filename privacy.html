Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Brush Bomb ("the game") is published by Brushwave Games, the studio name of Christopher Sterling, an individual developer based in Redmond, Washington, United States ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Brush Bomb apps for iPhone, iPad and Android and to this website.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. A Brush Bomb account is optional: if you create one, we store your email address, a password (encrypted), your friend code, invite rewards and a list of your signed-in devices, so you can sign in on other devices and get Paint for inviting friends. You can delete the account in the game at any time. We never ask for your name, phone number or contacts.
  • Sharing a score card or invite goes through your device's own share sheet. The game doesn't upload anything when you share.
  • If you send us art for #BrushBombArt, you send it from your own email. We may feature it in the game with credit, and you can ask us to remove it anytime.
  • Leaderboards on iPhone and iPad use Apple's Game Center, which is optional. Apple handles your scores and your Game Center name.
  • Your scores, settings, progress, Paint and Skips are saved only on your device. We don't receive them.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which can also fill ads from its mediation partners AppLovin and Unity Ads. These ad companies collect some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads, and on iPhone and iPad we ask permission before tracking. You can say no and still play everything.
  • Purchases are paid through Apple or Google. We never see your card details. Our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous record of each purchase so it can unlock what you bought.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device (in app storage): best scores and streaks, Daily Challenge results, scored attempts and day streak, daily missions progress, login reward days, unlocked #BrushBombArt artworks, the longest Chain Reaction, Gallery levels and stars, unlocked themes and theme trials, sound, music, announcer and vibration settings, an in-progress game so you can continue it, ad pacing counters (for example how many ads you've seen today and when the last one was shown), your Paint and Skip balance and a record of purchases already credited, your friend code and whether you have redeemed a friend's code, the creator code you entered (if any) and the date you entered it, and a cached copy of your Ad-free subscription status.

We don't upload this data to our own servers; we don't operate any. It is deleted when you delete the game. Your device's own backup feature (iCloud or Google backup), if you have it switched on, may include it under Apple's or Google's terms.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob and mediation partners

We use Google AdMob to show banner ads, full-screen ads between games, app-open ads, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. AdMob uses mediation, which means some ads may be supplied by these ad networks working with AdMob:

  • AppLovin (AppLovin Corporation): AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

When ads are requested or shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK and these partners' SDKs may collect and share the following, for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (not precise) location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and similar app-specific identifiers.
  • Device information, such as model, operating system, language, screen size and network type.
  • Interactions with ads and the game, such as app launches, ad views, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs, launch time and energy use.

Each ad company handles this data under its own privacy policy. For Google, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ad-free subscribers don't see banner, between-game or app-open ads, but rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch one, so the ad SDKs may still run.

3. Consent and tracking choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (consent form). Before ads load, the game shows Google's consent message, managed with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which is a certified consent management platform. You can accept or refuse personalized ads and choose purposes and partners. If you refuse, you may still get non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button on the game's home screen.
  • US state privacy laws. Where required, Google's consent tools also let you opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising.
  • iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency). The game asks permission before it, or the ad SDKs, can use your device's advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA isn't shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings (usually Google, Ads, or Privacy, Ads).
  • Offline. You can play without an internet connection. No ads load while offline.

4. In-app purchases: Apple, Google and RevenueCat

Brush Bomb offers optional in-app purchases: packs of Paint (the in-game currency) and the "Brush Bomb Ad-free" auto-renewing monthly subscription. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies (Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy). We don't receive your name, email address, Apple ID, Google account or payment card details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider to validate purchases and check subscription status. When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID created by the game, the store's receipt or purchase token, transaction details (product, price, currency, purchase, renewal, cancellation and refund dates), and basic technical context (platform, operating system and app version, country and language). RevenueCat uses this to unlock your purchases, prevent fraud and give us aggregated sales reports. If you enter a creator code (see section 5), the game also attaches that code, the month and date you entered it and the date its 12-month support period ends to the same anonymous ID at RevenueCat, so we can work out the creator's share of revenue; this happens even if you never buy anything. The game doesn't send RevenueCat your advertising ID, precise location or any account details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Paint and Skips have no cash value and are stored on your device; deleting the game deletes them. The subscription can be restored with "Restore purchases". Cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings (the game's "Manage subscription" button opens them). Deleting the game does not cancel it.

4a. Optional Brush Bomb account

You can create an account from the Account screen with your email address and a password. It is optional; everything in the game can be played without it. We use Supabase, Inc. as a service provider to host accounts (servers in the United States).

  • What we store: your email address; your password, stored only as a one-way encrypted hash; an account ID; the date you joined; your friend code; a random ID for each install you sign in from; for each signed-in device, a label such as "iPhone · Brush Bomb app", the platform, and when it signed in and was last used; and invite records (which friend code was used, by which account and install, when, and the Paint reward). Like most sign-in services, Supabase also records the IP address and browser or device type of sign-ins in security logs.
  • Why: to sign you in and keep you signed in on your devices, to let you see and sign out your devices, to send you sign-up and password reset codes by email, to give Paint to players whose friends join with their code, and to prevent fraud (for example the same install redeeming a code twice). We don't use your email for marketing, and account data isn't used for advertising or tracking or shared with ad companies.
  • Deleting it: Account screen → "Delete account" deletes your account, email address, devices and sessions right away. We keep an anonymous record that a friend code was used from that install (without your email or account) so the same install can't claim the invite reward again. You can also email us to delete your account.

5. Referral (friend) codes, creator codes and sharing

Each install creates a random friend code on the device. Without an account it isn't linked to you; if you sign in, it is saved with your account so friends' invites count for you. When you tap "Invite friends" or "Challenge a friend", the game opens your device's own share sheet with a short message, your friend code, a link and (for challenges) your score. You choose the app and the person; we don't see who you send it to, and the game never reads your contacts. When someone enters a friend code while signed in to a new account, the game sends the code and their random install ID to our account service (section 4a) so we can check it and reward the friend who invited them. Without an account, the code is only checked on their device.

Creator codes. Some creators share a code (for example CHOA) that you can enter once, in your first two weeks, under "Have a creator code?" for free Paint. Entering a code is optional. If you do, the code and the date are saved on your device and sent to RevenueCat with your anonymous ID (section 4). We use it only to calculate how much revenue from players who joined with that code we owe the creator under our agreement, for 12 months from the date you entered it. The creator doesn't receive your data, only totals. It isn't linked to your name or any account and isn't used for advertising or tracking.

Score cards and sharing

When you tap "Share image", "Challenge a friend" or "Invite friends", the game makes the score card picture on your device (your score, your board and a plain invite link) and hands it to your device's share sheet. You choose the app and the people. The game doesn't upload the picture or the message to us or anyone else. On iPhone and iPad, the picture is saved briefly in the app's temporary cache so the share sheet can read it.

6. #BrushBombArt submissions

You can send us your own art for the in-game #BrushBombArt gallery from the "Submit your art" screen. The button opens your own email app with a message to melodyxceo@gmail.com; nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and the game doesn't upload your art. We receive what you choose to send: your email address and name as they appear in your email, the art you attach, and the title and credit name you write. We use it only to review your art and, if we pick it, to feature it in Brush Bomb with the credit you asked for. By submitting, you confirm the art is yours and give us permission to show it in the game with credit. To have your art removed from the game, or your email deleted, email melodyxceo@gmail.com and we'll take it out in the next update. We keep submission emails only while we need them for this.

7. Game Center leaderboards (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, the Daily Challenge, Weekly High Score, All-Time Best and Longest Chain Reaction leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Game Center is optional; you can play everything without signing in. If you are signed in, the game sends your scores to Game Center, and Apple handles your scores, your Game Center name and your friends list under the Apple Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Apple ID, and we don't copy your scores or Game Center name to any server of ours (we don't run any). You can manage Game Center, including who can see you, in your device's Settings.

8. Analytics

We don't use a separate analytics, attribution or crash-reporting service in the game. The game keeps simple counters on your device (for example games played and ads seen) to pace ads and unlock features; they aren't sent to us. The ad companies and RevenueCat described above collect the limited analytics described in sections 2 and 4 for their services, and we may see aggregated, non-identifying reports from them (for example total ad impressions or sales by country). If we add an analytics service later, we'll update this policy before that version ships.

9. Children

Brush Bomb is a general-audience game that is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

10. How long data is kept, security and transfers

Data on your device is kept until you delete the game or clear its data. Account data, including the list of devices, is kept until you delete your account (section 4a). RevenueCat keeps purchase records while they are needed to provide your purchases, for accounting and tax, and for fraud prevention, unless we ask it to delete them sooner. Ad companies keep data according to their own policies. Data sent by the game to these services is encrypted in transit. These companies may process data in the United States and other countries, using legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or "sharing". Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalized ads and for tracking, on performance of a contract for purchases, and on legitimate interests for fraud prevention and keeping the game working.

Most choices are available directly: delete your account in the game, delete the game, use Privacy options, or change your tracking and ad settings. For purchase records held by RevenueCat on our behalf, email us and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete them. For data held by Google, AppLovin or Unity, use their controls or contact them through the links above. You can also contact us with any request, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the game.
