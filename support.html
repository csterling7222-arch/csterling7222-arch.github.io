Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop (or the Ad-free screen) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Restore brings back the Brush Bomb Ad-free subscription while it is active, on any of your devices.

Paint packs are consumable: once Paint is added to your balance it lives on that device, and the App Store and Google Play can't restore it. See "I reinstalled and lost my Paint" below.

How do I manage or cancel the Ad-free subscription?

In the game, open the Ad-free screen and tap Manage subscription. You can also do it directly:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Brush Bomb. Or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, Brush Bomb. Or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Ad-free until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription.

I reinstalled the game (or switched phones) and lost my Paint or progress.

Brush Bomb has no account or cloud save. Your scores, Gallery progress, themes, Paint and Skips are stored only on your device, so deleting the game deletes them, and we have no copy we can restore. A full device backup (iCloud or Google backup) restored to a phone may bring them back, but this isn't guaranteed.

What you can always get back: the Ad-free subscription, with Restore purchases.

If you were charged for a Paint pack but the Paint never appeared, first reopen the game while online (it finishes pending purchases on launch). If it still isn't there, email us with the order ID. Refunds are handled by the store: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, or your Google Play order history for Android.

Why do I see ads, and what does Ad-free remove?

Ads keep Brush Bomb free. We pace them carefully: no full-screen ads in your first few games, never in the middle of a run, and break ads only between games. The optional subscription Brush Bomb Ad-free ($4.99 a month in the US, local prices vary) removes banner, between-game and app-open ads, gives you revives, +5 moves and +1 Brush Bomb instantly with no video, and adds 200 Paint every month. Rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus stay available and optional.

How do I change my ad consent or tracking choice?
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: tap Privacy options at the bottom of the home screen to reopen Google's consent form and change your choices. The button appears where the consent rules apply.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, then turn Brush Bomb on or off.
  • Android: Settings, Google (or Privacy), Ads, to reset or delete your advertising ID.

Saying no never blocks any part of the game; ads may just be less relevant. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do friend codes work?

Tap Invite friends on the home screen to share your code. A new player can enter a friend's code once, within 7 days of first opening the game, and gets 100 Paint. Codes are checked on the device, so nothing is uploaded.

When does the Daily Challenge reset?

At midnight in your device's local time. Everyone gets the same pieces on the same date, so you can compare scores with friends.
